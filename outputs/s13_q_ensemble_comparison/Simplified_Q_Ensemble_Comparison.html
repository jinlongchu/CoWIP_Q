Simplified Independent Q Ensemble versus Full M4

Simplified = MADQN + global features + GRU + PI residual guidance + five-model independent Q ensemble. Relative to full M4, remove QMIX and use independent local TD targets; replace 3-step with 1-step; replace PER with uniform replay.

This is a new evaluation of existing checkpoints, not retraining. Each of the five old S13 members trained for 300 episodes. Inspection found a direct-action mask applied to residual outputs in the GRU branch, and no zero-residual output-bias initialization matching M4. These results are not a strictly controlled three-module removal ablation. The second row corrects only the execution mask, leaving historical effects in the training targets.

Action clipping and environment cap bounds still prevent limit violations, but introduce residual-action aliases, omit legal candidates, and make training/exploration masks inconsistent. This implementation-semantic issue cannot be ignored.

How to Use These Results

This evaluates the existing global-feature + GRU + PI-residual model with five-model Q ensembling. It does not determine whether a corrected simplified version beats full M4 under matched training and selection. That requires residual-mask fixes, matched initialization/budgets and retraining.

The historical test/holdout results had been inspected during development. Additional seeds were fixed before this evaluation and were not used for tuning. They still use the same operating schedule and do not establish generalization across schedules or factories. All metrics come from closed-loop runs. ± is the sample standard deviation across seeds, not a confidence interval. A cap-change point is a decision at which at least one cap changes.
